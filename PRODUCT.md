# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Presenters preparing to show a sample deck, a local PDF, or a supported Canva presentation. They use GestureDeck on a computer and may use a phone as a slide remote.

## Product Purpose

GestureDeck is a local-first presentation workspace. It helps presenters start a deck quickly, navigate it from the computer or phone, and understand how to connect a Canva presentation. Success means that the presenter can tell what to do next and whether the selected presentation is ready.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, and warm. The interface should feel composed and dependable before and during a presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded control panels, hidden primary actions, ambiguous connection states, decorative effects that distract from presenting, and setup instructions that assume prior knowledge.

## Design Principles

- Keep the presentation and next action visually primary.
- Explain Canva setup in clear steps and show connection readiness in plain language.
- Keep status and recovery actions visible when pairing or reconnecting.
- Preserve the local-first privacy boundary for presentation files.
- Use one consistent, accessible visual language across the landing page, workspace, phone remote, and extension popup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader status updates, and reduced-motion support. Keep text readable and phone controls easy to tap. Maintain sufficient contrast for text, controls, and connection states.
